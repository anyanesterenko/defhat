01/05 About

An engineering team that takes technical challenges all the way to results

DefHat is a technology company working at the intersection of engineering, IT and supply. We take on complex technical requests — from requirements analysis and technology sourcing to development, integration and ongoing support.

Our team combines expertise in hardware and software development, data processing and artificial intelligence, communications, positioning and measurement systems, as well as international technology trade and logistics.

For our clients this means one accountable partner instead of several contractors: fewer hand-offs, transparent stages and a solution that works in your environment — not just on paper.

  • 02

Approach

Precision at the component level. Integrity at the system level.

We work with technology from a single board to an integrated system — checking, measuring and testing before a solution is handed over.
